# Security

Factory Pack reads files on your computer and sends things to a server, so it's held to a simple promise: it sends only what the game writes about itself and pictures out of the game and the mods you picked, and it never reads anything else. The README's [Is it safe?](README.md#is-it-safe) section says how that's kept.

If you find a way to make it read or send anything it shouldn't, or anything else that puts people's computers at risk, please report it privately: on this repository's **Security** tab, press **Report a vulnerability**. That reaches me without anyone else seeing it, and I'll get back to you.

For anything else, an ordinary [issue](https://github.com/DuffleOne/factory-pack/issues) is the place.
